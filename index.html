Privacy Policy for Turns

Developer: MindOops Studio
Package name: com.mindoops.turns
App version covered: 1.0
Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio handles information in connection with Turns, a single-player puzzle game for Android.

Turns does not require players to create an account, log in, submit personal forms, or provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, payment information, or precise location information inside the game.

1. Information Players Provide

Turns does not ask players to directly provide personal information during normal gameplay. The game does not include account registration, social login, chat, player profiles, or forms for submitting personal details.

The game does not ask players to provide:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Precise location data
  • Payment information
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, audio recordings, or files
  • User-generated content

2. Local Game Data

Turns may store gameplay progress, level status, preferences, settings, and similar game data locally on the player's device. This local data is used to let the game remember progress and settings.

MindOops Studio does not maintain a server-side player account database for Turns. Local game data can be removed by clearing the app storage/data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Device, Diagnostics, and Technical Data

Turns is built with the Unity game engine and is distributed through Google Play. As part of app delivery, security, compatibility, crash diagnostics, performance monitoring, and platform operation, Google Play, Android, Unity, device manufacturers, or operating system services may process technical information.

This technical information may include:

  • Device or other identifiers
  • App version and package information
  • Device model, operating system version, and language/region settings
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and performance information
  • Information needed for app installation, updates, security, and abuse prevention

MindOops Studio does not use this technical information to identify individual players for advertising, profiling, or selling personal information. Some of this data may be collected or shared by Google Play, Android, Unity, or device services under their own privacy policies.

4. App Permissions

Turns may request or include standard Android permissions required for the app to work.

  • Internet access may be included by the game engine, platform services, or to open external links such as this Privacy Policy.
  • Vibration may be used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay.

Turns does not use these permissions to collect names, contacts, photos, videos, audio files, precise location, payment details, or user-generated content.

5. Advertising, Analytics, and Purchases

The current Android version of Turns does not show third-party ads and does not include in-app purchases.

Turns does not use advertising identifiers for ad targeting, ad measurement, profiling, or marketing. MindOops Studio does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers, ad networks, social networks, or data brokers.

Platform-level or engine-level technical services may still process diagnostics, crash, performance, security, or device identifier information as described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

MindOops Studio does not sell player data. Data may be processed by third-party platform or service providers only as needed for app distribution, security, diagnostics, crash reporting, performance, support, or legal compliance.

These providers may include:

  • Google Play and Android services
  • Unity engine or Unity-related services
  • Device manufacturers and operating system services

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress and settings remain on the player's device until the player clears app data or uninstalls Turns.

MindOops Studio does not create player accounts and does not maintain a server-side account database for Turns. If a player contacts MindOops Studio by email, that email and related support information may be retained only as long as needed to respond, provide support, keep business records, or meet legal obligations.

Players may request privacy-related help by contacting MindOops Studio at the email address listed below. Platform-level data handled by Google, Android, Unity, or device services is controlled according to those providers' own privacy policies and user controls.

8. Children's Privacy

Turns is intended for a general audience. MindOops Studio does not knowingly ask children to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location data, payment information, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, or other directly provided personal information through the game.

9. Security

MindOops Studio takes reasonable steps to protect information associated with the operation and support of Turns. However, no app, device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. External Links

Turns may open external links, such as this Privacy Policy. External websites and platform services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MindOops Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Turns changes. If material changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be posted with a new effective date.
