Privacy Policy for Turns

Developer: MindOops Studio
Package name: com.mindoops.turns
App version covered: 1.0 and later Android updates unless replaced by a newer policy
Effective date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio handles information in connection with Turns, a single-player puzzle game for Android.

Turns does not require players to create an account, log in, submit personal forms, or provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, payment information, or precise location information inside the game.

1. Information Players Provide

Turns does not ask players to directly provide personal information during normal gameplay. The game does not include account registration, social login, chat, player profiles, or forms for submitting personal details.

The game does not ask players to provide:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Precise location data
  • Payment information
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, audio recordings, or files
  • User-generated content

2. Local Game Data

Turns may store gameplay progress, level status, preferences, settings, notification preferences, and similar game data locally on the player's device. This local data is used to let the game remember progress and settings.

MindOops Studio does not maintain a server-side player account database for Turns. Local game data can be removed by clearing the app storage/data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Device, Diagnostics, and Technical Data

Turns is built with the Unity game engine and is distributed through Google Play. As part of app delivery, security, compatibility, crash diagnostics, performance monitoring, and platform operation, Google Play, Android, Unity, device manufacturers, or operating system services may process technical information.

This technical information may include:

  • Device or other identifiers
  • App version, package name, install, and update information
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and region settings
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and other app performance data
  • Information needed for app installation, updates, security, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention

MindOops Studio does not use this technical information to identify individual players for advertising, profiling, or selling personal information. Some technical data may be collected automatically by Google Play, Android, Unity, or device services under their own privacy policies and controls.

4. App Permissions

Turns may request or include standard Android permissions required for the app to work.

  • Internet access may be included by the game engine, platform services, app security, diagnostics, app updates, or to open external links such as this Privacy Policy.
  • Vibration may be used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Notification permission may be requested on supported Android versions if Turns offers reminders, return-to-game messages, progress reminders, or similar app notifications.

Turns does not use these permissions to collect names, contacts, photos, videos, audio files, precise location, payment details, or user-generated content.

5. Notifications

Turns may use local or platform-supported notifications to remind players about the game, progress, or app-related updates. Notifications are optional and can be allowed, blocked, or managed through Android system settings.

MindOops Studio does not use notifications for third-party advertising, ad targeting, profiling, or selling personal information. Notification delivery may involve Android, Google Play, Unity, device manufacturers, or operating system services processing technical information needed to display or manage notifications.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Purchases

The current Android version of Turns does not show third-party ads and does not include in-app purchases.

Turns does not use advertising identifiers for ad targeting, ad measurement, profiling, or marketing. MindOops Studio does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers, ad networks, social networks, or data brokers.

Platform-level or engine-level technical services may still process diagnostics, crash, performance, security, or device identifier information as described in this Privacy Policy.

7. Data Sharing and Service Providers

MindOops Studio does not sell player data. Data may be processed by third-party platform, engine, operating system, or service providers only as needed for app distribution, security, diagnostics, crash reporting, performance, notifications, support, or legal compliance.

These providers may include:

  • Google Play and Android services
  • Unity engine or Unity-related services
  • Device manufacturers and operating system services

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress, settings, and notification preferences remain on the player's device until the player clears app data or uninstalls Turns.

Because Turns does not create player accounts and MindOops Studio does not maintain a server-side player account database for Turns, MindOops Studio generally cannot remotely delete local game data stored only on a player's device. Players can delete local game data by clearing app storage/data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

If a player contacts MindOops Studio by email, that email and related support information may be retained only as long as needed to respond, provide support, keep business records, or meet legal obligations. Players may request deletion of support emails or other information they directly sent to MindOops Studio by using the contact email below.

Platform-level data handled by Google, Android, Unity, or device services is controlled according to those providers' own privacy policies and user controls.

9. Children's Privacy

Turns is intended for a general audience. MindOops Studio does not knowingly ask children to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location data, payment information, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, or other directly provided personal information through the game.

10. Security

MindOops Studio takes reasonable steps to protect information associated with the operation and support of Turns. When technical data is transmitted by platform or service providers, it is expected to be handled through secure connections where applicable. However, no app, device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. External Links

Turns may open external links, such as this Privacy Policy. External websites and platform services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MindOops Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Turns changes. If material changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be posted with a new effective date.
