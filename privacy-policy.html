Privacy Policy for Turns

Developer: MindOops
Package name: com.mindoops.turns
Effective date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops handles information in connection with Turns, a single-player puzzle game for Android.

1. Information We Collect

Turns does not ask players to create an account, log in, submit forms, or directly provide personal information inside the game.

The game does not ask players to provide:

  • names
  • email addresses
  • phone numbers
  • precise location data
  • payment information
  • contacts
  • photos, videos, audio recordings, or files
  • user-generated content

The current Android version of Turns does not include advertising SDKs, social login SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, in-app purchases, or third-party account services.

2. App Permissions and Device Information

Turns may include standard Android permissions, such as internet access and vibration. Vibration is used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay.

Internet access may be included by the game engine or used to open external links such as this Privacy Policy. MindOops does not use these permissions to collect names, contact details, precise location, payment information, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, or user-generated content.

3. Platform and Store Services

When you install, update, or use Turns through Google Play or an Android device, Google, Android, Unity, device manufacturers, or operating-system services may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

This may include information used for app delivery, security, diagnostics, crash reporting, performance, abuse prevention, or store operations. MindOops does not sell this information and does not use it for advertising.

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Third Parties

Turns does not show third-party ads in the current Android version.

Turns does not use third-party advertising identifiers for ad targeting, ad measurement, profiling, or marketing in the current Android version.

Turns does not share personal information with third-party advertisers, ad networks, social networks, or data brokers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Turns does not create player accounts and does not maintain a server-side player account database.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored game data by using your device's app settings to clear storage/data for Turns or by uninstalling the game.

6. Children's Privacy

Turns is intended for a general audience. MindOops does not knowingly ask children to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, or other directly provided personal information through the game.

7. Security

MindOops takes reasonable steps to protect information associated with the operation and support of Turns. However, no app, device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MindOops may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Turns changes. If material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.
